Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly stores the expense data you voluntarily enter, including transaction amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic account information such as your email address when you register.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking features of Spendly. This includes organizing your expenses by category, generating spending summaries, and providing filtered views of your financial data.

3. Data Storage

All your financial data is stored locally on your device or on our servers. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use third-party services for hosting and infrastructure, but these providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your data at any time through your account settings. If you wish to delete all your data, contact us through the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app.
